EMDR Therapy Guide

EMDR Healing Session

Eye Movement Desensitization and Reprocessing (EMDR) is a structured therapy that encourages the patient to focus briefly on trauma memories while experiencing bilateral stimulation (typically eye movements), reducing the vividness and emotion associated with the trauma.

Important Notice: This application serves as a self-help tool. It is not a substitute for professional medical advice, diagnosis, or psychotherapy. Ensure you are in a quiet, safe, and comfortable environment before starting.

Bring the Memory to Mind

Close your eyes or focus on the screen. Bring the distressing or traumatic memory to your awareness. Allow the thoughts, feelings, and body sensations associated with this event to arise.

Rate Current Distress

Focusing on that memory, rate the intensity of your current emotional distress or pain on a scale from 1 to 10.

Select a distress rating

Sensory Details: Images

Focus on the memory. In the box below, describe what images, mental pictures, or visual scenes you see when this traumatic memory is triggered.

Sensory Details: Sounds

Focus on the memory. Write down what voices, spoken words, or specific sounds you heard during this traumatic event.

Sensory Details: Noises

Focus on the memory. Describe any sudden noises, background noises, background hums, or sharp sounds associated with the scene.

Sensory Details: Tactile

Focus on the memory. Describe the physical sensations you felt or now feel in your body (e.g., muscle tension, pressure, temperature, heartbeat, shivering).

Sensory Details: Tastes

Focus on the memory. If any, write down what tastes or gustatory sensations you recall. (This step is optional - you can leave it blank).

Sensory Details: Smells

Focus on the memory. Describe what odors, scents, chemical smells, or fragrances are associated with this memory.

Take a Deep Breath

The tracking set is complete. Now, take a very deep breath (as deep as possible). Hold it for a moment, and then exhale slowly, releasing any remaining tension.

Inhale

Re-evaluate Distress Level
